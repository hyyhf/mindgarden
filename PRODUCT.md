# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React 18, TypeScript, CSS Modules, and bounded Three.js scenes inside the DeepSeek Harness Web plugin shell.

## Users

Chinese-speaking DeepSeek Harness users who want a private place for companion dialogue, reflection, memory governance, and long-horizon self-understanding without being diagnosed, scored, or pressured to manufacture progress.

## Product Purpose

Mind Garden is a private companion and reflection workspace. It helps a person notice the present, hold unresolved concerns, preserve chosen memories, review time, explore real-life questions, and revisit personal principles while keeping every interpretation and action under that person's control.

Success means the user feels gently received, can immediately understand what is available, can complete common actions with less friction, and can always see what is private, model-derived, pending confirmation, or ready to return to the Harness conversation.

## Positioning

Mind Garden combines ordinary Harness conversation with encrypted, evidence-bound, user-governed reflection records. Model suggestions remain candidates until the user confirms them; no neighboring generic journal or wellness dashboard can truthfully copy that mechanism without the same vault, provenance, permission, and composer-handoff boundaries.

## Operating Context

The product runs as an installable workspace inside DeepSeek Harness Web. People move between the resident conversation and nine existing reflection spaces: Today, Concerns, Calendar, Photo Story, Memory, Growth, Constellation, Life Review, and Philosophy. The redesign may group these spaces into five clearer regions, but every existing capability, state, and user-owned record remains available.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve and strengthen all nine spaces; navigation grouping must not delete, hide, or downgrade their functions.
- Preserve encrypted storage, backup, authenticated restore, legacy migration, key rotation, provider boundaries, and the explicit consent gates around model and image observation.
- Preserve memory provenance, confirmation, revision, deletion, sensitivity, recall controls, and candidate-versus-confirmed distinctions.
- Preserve explicit record-to-composer handoff; the plugin never auto-sends a garden record or creates a parallel conversation surface.
- Preserve real-record fidelity: no fabricated memories, dates, activity, scores, diagnoses, personality verdicts, or personal claims for visual density.
- Preserve complete loading, empty, error, permission, disabled, pending, success, migration, and recovery states.
- Strengthen information hierarchy, action discoverability, state clarity, keyboard use, responsive behavior, and reduced-motion equivalence.
- Use Three.js only where depth carries product meaning; ordinary records and settings remain fast, legible HTML.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: Mind Garden / 心智庭院.
- Chinese-first product language with a warm, restrained companion voice.
- Approved replacement visual world: “晨光造物室” — a fresh, warm New Chinese visual language built from morning light, warm cream and ivory architectural fields, xuan-paper diffusion, pale wood, limestone, white porcelain, misted glass, restrained brass, deep ink typography, indigo-led actions, and green reserved for sparse semantic states.
- New Chinese styling must feel contemporary and breathable rather than retro or ornamental: avoid heavy ebony, dominant black, red-and-gold luxury codes, generic ink-wash overlays, lattice pasted onto every surface, and decorative piles of bamboo, lotus, plum blossom, lantern, or cloud motifs.
- The experience should feel refined, elegant, warm, materially authored, and spatially memorable without becoming a generic cream wellness page, a literal cute botanical garden, or a neon cosmic cockpit.
- Every existing function is preserved and made clearer and easier to use.

## Product Principles

1. Preserve capability, reduce cognitive load.
2. The user owns every conclusion and every transition into conversation.
3. Warmth comes from material, pacing, and humane language—not decorative softness or hidden complexity.
4. Spatial effects clarify relationships; they never replace semantic controls or evidence.
5. Empty and uncertain states remain honest, calm, and actionable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The web UI must preserve semantic headings and controls, visible keyboard focus, complete keyboard navigation, readable contrast and type sizes, reduced-motion equivalents, non-WebGL fallbacks, and responsive layouts that do not require horizontally scrolling through all nine spaces.
