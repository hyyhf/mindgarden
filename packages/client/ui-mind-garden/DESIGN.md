# Design System: Mind Garden / 心智庭院

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Personal Orrery"**

Mind Garden is a living private observatory inside the DeepSeek Harness shell. The host supplies compact controls, semantic color, spacing, and one outline-icon family; the garden adds sunlit plaster, a dark graphite rail, smoked teal glass, aged brass calibration, an oxblood grounding action, and a restrained serif voice for its most intimate statement.

Today opens inside a full-height personal orrery: six truthful continuity nodes occupy an implied orbit around the grounding actions, one sparse calibration field preserves instrument depth without drawing across the copy, the housing tilts and relights with the pointer, and an integrated glass ledger carries the current question and saved review. Concerns uses a deep-green private placement table, while Calendar joins a warm paper atlas to a day or trend ledger. Memory becomes a user-governed archive room with truthful recall and candidate registers; Growth becomes a warm field journal joined to a compact reality instrument and a deep-green experiment desk. Life Review becomes a chronological evidence corridor, and Philosophy becomes a specimen room that keeps contemplation, inert proposals, living principles, counterexamples, and revision history visibly distinct. Constellation and Photo Story retain their deeper WebGL and particle experiences with semantic HTML controls and complete reduced-motion states.

The canonical composition is [Personal Orrery](.impeccable/mocks/decision/personal-orrery.webp); its [prompt and provenance sidecar](.impeccable/mocks/decision/personal-orrery.webp.json) and [surface brief](.impeccable/surface-brief.md) preserve the design contract. Four generated rasters carry exact prompts and JSON provenance: [Warm Garden Orrery](src/assets/garden-orrery-warm.webp), [Personal Orrery Stage](src/assets/personal-orrery-stage.webp), [Garden Threshold](src/assets/garden-threshold-warm.webp), and [Photo Story Empty](src/assets/photo-story-empty-warm.webp). Warm Garden Orrery is the active Today material plate; authored HTML, SVG, CSS 3D, and WebGL own every label, control, orbit, node, particle, and state.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Harness-native shell with a dark instrument rail and a light reading plane, both derived from semantic color.
- Full-height Today orrery built from open questions, saved reviews, and honest unfilled positions.
- Nine spaces represented by one 20 × 20, 1.45-stroke outline grammar.
- Flat records and controls below a tactile Today stage; full WebGL depth remains concentrated in Constellation and Photo Story.
- Graphite housing, smoked teal glass, aged brass calibration, warm dust, and an oxblood grounding action.
- Warm, materially textured image thresholds and stage plates with repository-local prompts and provenance.
- A private concern placement table and tactile time atlas that preserve edit, filter, and composer-handoff workflows.
- A memory archive and reality field journal that make provenance, correction, observation, and user authority feel tangible.
- A life time corridor and philosophy specimen room that keep retrospective evidence, revisable principles, and explicit composer handoff intact.
- Explicit consent before frozen-image observation, followed by grounded and revisable dialogue.
- An authenticated archive recovery ceremony that separates file selection, inspection, merge preview, explicit confirmation, and durable receipt without ornamental separators; original archives receive a visibly narrower photo-conversion scope.
- One clear heading per focused surface, with redundant eyebrow or kicker labels removed.
- Keyboard-contained Harness settings that return focus to their invoking control.
- Responsive reflow, visible focus, reduced-motion equivalence, and explicit rendering fallbacks.

## Colors

Routine records and controls inherit Harness semantic aliases so light and dark themes preserve meaning. Three bounded immersive worlds use stable art palettes: the warm garden observatory combines graphite, smoked teal, aged brass, oxblood, and sunlit plaster; Constellation uses a deep green-black sky with brass, teal, blue, and earthen-red signals; Photo Story uses warm darkroom black, paper ivory, amber, and ember. These palettes stop at their owning surface, while error, focus, permission, and destructive states retain semantic meaning.

### Primary

- **Precise Action Blue:** Activates navigation, links, focus rings, editable controls, question nodes, and other user-governed actions; it is a signal, not a field color.

### Secondary

- **Observation Green:** Identifies continuity, privacy, confirmed or review-related states, and the observatory's green-teal geometry.

### Tertiary

- **Calibration Brass:** Marks review nodes, orbital calibration, and caution or timing accents without becoming decorative gold.
- **Oxblood Action:** Marks the single grounding action inside the Today aperture; destructive feedback continues to use the unmixed error role.
- **Error Red:** Appears only for failed operations and destructive-state feedback.

### Neutral

- **Editorial Ground:** Owns the workspace and form surfaces.
- **Navigation Field:** Separates the local rail from the workspace through a quiet plane and hairline, not a shadow.
- **Ink / Secondary Ink / Quiet Ink:** Form the reading hierarchy from primary content through descriptions and metadata.
- **Hairline:** Divides editorial sequences, record rows, field edges, and structural seams.

### Named Rules

**The Host Palette Rule.** Routine UI consumes Harness semantic aliases. Literal art palettes are allowed only inside the Today instrument, Constellation sky, and Photo Story darkroom, where a fixed world is required for image and WebGL contrast; semantic focus, error, and permission states remain explicit.

**The Calibration Accent Rule.** Blue, green, and brass identify action or record meaning; never wash an ordinary section in accent color or use brass as ornamental luxury.

**The Secondary Ink Floor Rule.** Supporting instructions, timestamps, field labels, observer boundaries, and other useful auxiliary copy resolve to secondary ink; quiet or caption ink is reserved for truly optional atmosphere that can disappear without hiding meaning.

### Hierarchy

- **Display:** The opening Today statement and largest immersive-space title use the 32–52 px responsive role; the 28–44 px immersive role and 21–29 px editorial lead step down in compact spatial contexts. Keep one dominant heading per view.

- **Title / Control:** 13 px titles anchor navigation and actions; the 14 px control role appears where photo-card or compact action copy needs one step more presence. A 17 px role is reserved for icon-like action glyphs, never prose.
- **Body / Supporting:** 13 px body copy carries reflective prompts and records; 12 px supporting copy carries boundaries, helper text, and field labels. Long reading lines stop around 65–72 characters where the layout permits.
- **Metadata Ramp:** 11 px labels, 10 px metadata, and 9 px captions handle counts, dates, sources, statuses, and calibration. The 8 px micro and rare 7 px trace roles exist only inside dense spatial observation or provenance surfaces; never use them for required instructions, primary controls, or paragraph copy.

### Named Rules

**The Instrument Scale Rule.** One serif statement leads the Today aperture; all operational hierarchy returns to Harness sans. Section headings orient, body copy explains, metadata recedes, and repeated eyebrows never restate the heading.

## Layout

The desktop shell places a 216 px graphite instrument rail beside the workspace; the rail can collapse to 58 px without changing the nine-space information architecture. Today uses a warm full-height stage capped at 1440 px: the orrery occupies the main aperture and the current question and saved review sit in an integrated right ledger. Check-in and journal instruments continue below on a 1240 px warm writing plane. Named container queries adapt these plugin-owned layouts to the space left by Harness rails instead of assuming that viewport width equals workspace width. The inactive-garden threshold and empty Photo Story archive use unequal two-column compositions so concise actions stay quieter than their warm no-text rasters.

Workspace inline padding scales from 20 px to 58 px, major column gaps from 22 px to 64 px, and section rhythm follows the spacing tokens rather than a card grid. At 900 px the Today opening and content columns stack; at 820 px the garden rail becomes a horizontally scrollable icon strip; compact photo and form layouts reflow between 720 px and 480 px, while the narrowest workspace padding becomes 14–16 px. The rail's Constellation call to action is data-aware—ritual, new dust, continue, or draw—while the footer opens a Harness-native settings sheet for mode and intent rather than introducing a separate plugin settings language.

**The Open Plane Rule.** Use whitespace, tonal planes, and typography to sequence content; a collection of floating rounded cards or bento cells is not the Mind Garden layout.

**The Separator Restraint Rule.** A horizontal rule must encode a real grid, input, state, or disclosure boundary. Do not underline headings, stack repeated row rules, or stretch ornamental hairlines across an otherwise open plane.

## Elevation & Depth

Ordinary spaces are flat and structurally layered by tone, border, and spacing. Today is the deliberate exception: a generated material plate sits behind authored concentric SVG rotors, CSS 3D brass and enamel layers, truthful semantic nodes, pointer-driven tilt, moving specular light, and slow dust. The pointer path is animation-frame throttled, offscreen content remains ordinary document flow, and reduced motion freezes every ambient layer without removing records or actions.

Constellation owns the product's live spatial graph: lit emissive nodes and shells, selected-node rings, shader-driven batched tube links, three orbital rings, fog, hemisphere and point lighting, and two deterministic shader star layers. The renderer clears a stable #07130f deep-sky plane at 0.82 alpha; the center uses #e2b34f, review nodes use #69b998, question and continuity signals use #83a8ff and #8eb6ae, and trait nodes use #b36a52. Near and far dust use 2.05 / 2.8 point sizes at 0.90 / 0.78 opacity; continuity and ordinary tubes use 0.20 / 0.13 radii. Raycast picking, drag inertia, damped wheel zoom, and a semantic companion selector make the field tactile without making 3D position the only source of meaning. Photo Story owns the other deep surface: a warm #0d0b10 darkroom, a pointer-draggable CSS 3D ring with inertial snapping, and a sampled Three.js particle reconstruction inside an opened story. The particle scene can scatter and recompose the verified image while preserving its color and silhouette.

Both WebGL surfaces adapt device pixel ratio to hardware and observed frame time, pause when the document is hidden or the surface leaves the viewport, and fully release observers, listeners, frames, geometries, materials, and textures on teardown. Reduced motion switches Constellation to on-demand rendering and Photo Story to a stable semantic view; the original image remains the verified fallback when particle rendering cannot run.

### Named Rules

**The Deep-Sky Constellation Rule.** Keep the theme-isolated #07130f clear plane at 0.82 alpha; render ivory orbit dust at 2.05 / 0.90, question-blue near dust at 2.8 / 0.78, and continuity / ordinary tubes at 0.20 / 0.13 radii so every WebGL signal survives both Host themes.

**The Depth Reservation Rule.** Today uses bounded CSS 3D for one tactile instrument; Constellation and Photo Story own continuous WebGL depth. Routine forms, records, navigation, and settings stay flat.

**The Motion Equivalence Rule.** Reduced motion removes ambient particles, autoplay, orbit drift, and continuous WebGL rendering; Photo Story becomes one stable active card, Constellation renders on demand, and a verified original image or complete node list preserves the content when WebGL is unavailable.

## Shapes

The form language is square to lightly eased. Three- and 4 px corners are reserved for precision-instrument seams; 5 px corners fit nested segmented selections and framed prints; 6 px corners fit compact controls; 7 px corners fit the local rail; 8 px corners fit fields, primary actions, and focused overlays; 9–10 px corners soften observation gates and writing instruments. The 11 px radius is the upper bound for routine UI and the bounded Today stage. A 999 px pill is reserved for small visible-element chips, while true circles remain limited to orbital nodes, status lights, stars, and other genuinely radial data.

Hairline borders and open edges carry most grouping. The nine garden-space icons share a 20 × 20 view box, 1.45 px rounded stroke, no fill, and current color; they render at 18 px in the rail and remain recognizable when the rail collapses.

**The Outline Family Rule.** Every garden navigation glyph uses the same compact rounded-line grammar, and section actions reuse Harness outline primitives; do not mix emoji, Unicode marks, filled pictograms, icon fonts, or unrelated stroke weights.

## Components

### Buttons

- **Primary:** A compact 40 px action with a precise filled state, short label, and clear disabled feedback.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent at rest and blue or secondary-ink led; hover adds only a quiet Harness interaction field.
- **Focus:** A 2 px action-blue outline with a 2 px offset remains visible on every actionable control.

### Inputs / Fields

- **Style:** Editorial-ground fill, one hairline border, lightly eased corners, inherited type, and comfortable 11 × 12 px text inset.
- **Focus:** The border changes to action blue without layout movement.
- **Error / Disabled:** Nearby localized status names the failure; disabled controls retain their form and reduce opacity rather than disappearing.

### Navigation

The local rail groups all nine established spaces: Today, Concerns, Calendar, Photo Story, Memory, Growth, Constellation, Life Review, and Philosophy. The active item uses a 2 px oxblood marker, pale label, and a restrained brass-and-oxblood field; on mobile the same buttons form a horizontal strip with the marker below, preserving labels and keyboard semantics.

### Harness Settings Sheet

The rail footer opens the profile control room as a modal sheet on the Harness plane. Opening moves focus into the sheet; Tab and Shift+Tab remain inside; Escape, the close control, or the scrim dismisses it; dismissal returns focus to the exact footer trigger without scrolling. The close control and every nested action keep the standard 2 px visible focus ring.

Archive recovery sits inside one smoked-teal instrument bay, not a stack of bordered cards. File selection and passphrase entry lead to an authenticated preview that reports records to add, same-id records to keep, verified photo count, and package size. A separate confirmation performs the non-overwriting merge; errors retain the inputs for correction or retry, and success replaces the preview with a compact durable receipt. Tonal planes, spacing, icon changes, and state copy establish hierarchy; no long rule or decorative underline separates the stages.

### Personal Orrery

The Today instrument always has six positions around an implied orbit. A sparse rotating calibration field and material depth establish the instrument while the center stays free of axes, connector curves, and layered concentric guides. It prefers up to three real open questions and three real saved reviews; remaining positions use localized invitations such as “waiting to be named” or “not yet written,” never fabricated memories, times, or activity. The center contains real grounding actions and privacy state, the right ledger projects the current question and saved review, and the caption reports actual record counts. Pointer tilt and relighting are supplemental; touch and reduced-motion users retain the complete static instrument.

### Concern Placement Table

The Heart Basket is a single private writing instrument rather than a stack of generic cards. A deep-green compose plane holds the concern and its observed date; saved concerns continue as woven record rows with explicit edit, complete, convert, and composer-handoff actions. Editing remains in place so context is not lost, while status and date stay readable without depending on color.

### Time Atlas

Calendar uses a warm paper month grid joined to a deep-green ledger. Previous, next, and today controls preserve orientation; semantic filters select real projected record kinds; day and trend modes change the ledger without changing the month. Each projected record can be deliberately placed in the resident Harness composer, but the plugin never sends it automatically.

### Memory Archive

Memory governance begins with one deep-green archive aperture and three truthful registers: active, awaiting decision, and unresolved relationship counts. Recall audit, extraction, automation permission, candidate review, provenance, revision, and deletion remain explicit HTML controls. Confirmed memories can be deliberately placed in the resident composer as revisable background; candidates never cross that boundary before confirmation.

### Reality Field Journal

Growth pairs a small orbit instrument with a deep-green experiment desk and a warm chronological journal. The instrument reports only active and observed record counts. Journal rows preserve hypotheses, actions, and observations without scores, streaks, synthetic progress, or verdicts. Each experiment can be started, observed, stopped, or carried into the resident composer by an explicit action.

### Life Time Corridor

Life Review turns saved period reviews into a warm chronological corridor rather than a summary dashboard. The horizon instrument reports only the truthful review count; each material plane preserves the verified period, status, source state, and user-authored interpretation. A review can be deliberately placed in the resident Harness composer to revisit what still holds, but it is never sent automatically.

### Philosophy Specimen Room

Philosophy separates three epistemic states through structure and material: confirmed contemplations remain on an open reading plane, AI proposals wait inside a deep-green decision cabinet, and user-governed principles live as warm numbered folios. Every principle keeps its formation context, direct quote, counterexample, scope, mutable status, and expandable version history. Confirmed contemplations and principles can return to the resident composer only through an explicit action.

### Records and Empty States

Questions, reviews, calendar events, memories, growth observations, and philosophy material use flat rows with explicit status, date, source, and action affordances. Empty states state exactly what is absent, avoid pressure to manufacture content, and offer a real next action only where one exists.

### Photo Story

The dynamic album exposes previous, pause or play, and next controls, announces position, pauses autoplay during pointer or keyboard attention, and keeps only the active card in the tab order through roving focus. Left and right arrows move the active frame and focus together; pointer drag gains inertia before snapping to a real card; reduced motion disables autoplay and presents one stable active card.
